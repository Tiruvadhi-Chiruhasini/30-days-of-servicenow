# Day 7: Incident Management

Today I explored Incident Management, one of the core modules of ServiceNow.

An Incident is created when something is broken or not functioning as expected.

Examples:

* VPN not working
* Laptop won't start
* Email service unavailable

The goal of Incident Management is not to investigate deeply but to restore the service as quickly as possible.

Typical lifecycle:

New
→ In Progress
→ Resolved
→ Closed

### What clicked for me

At first, an Incident looked like just another ticket.

But I realized an Incident is actually a way of tracking disruptions and ensuring users are not left without support.

### My takeaway

An Incident is a symptom.

The immediate goal is getting the service back up and running.
