# Day 8: Problem Management

Today I learned the difference between fixing an issue and understanding why it keeps happening.

Imagine receiving the same Incident repeatedly:

* VPN down
* VPN down
* VPN down

Eventually the question becomes:

"Why is this happening again and again?"

That is where Problem Management comes in.

Instead of resolving symptoms, it focuses on identifying the root cause.

### What clicked for me

Incidents may look separate on the surface, but they can often point to a single underlying problem.

### My takeaway

Incidents fight the fire.

Problems find out what started it.
