# Day 11: Knowledge Management

Today I explored Knowledge Management.

Organizations receive many repeated questions:

* How do I reset my password?
* How do I connect to VPN?
* How do I request software?

Instead of answering the same questions repeatedly, companies create Knowledge Articles.

These articles become a self-service resource for users.

### What clicked for me

Good documentation saves time for both support teams and users.

It turns repeated explanations into reusable knowledge.

### My takeaway

Documentation is one of the most underrated forms of problem solving.
